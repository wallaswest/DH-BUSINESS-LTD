About us

A dependable supply partner for Rwanda's tenders

DH Business Rwanda Ltd is a Kigali‑based company supplying goods and non‑consultant works to public and private sector organisations through the tender process.

What we do

We respond to tenders and direct procurement requests across eight core categories — food and catering, stationery, biological assets, medical and veterinary supplies, agricultural inputs, construction materials, furniture, and kitchen materials.

Rather than specialising narrowly, we've built relationships with suppliers and transporters across these categories so that institutions can source multiple requirements through one registered, accountable company instead of managing several vendors.

How we work

Every tender we take on is assessed against our ability to deliver on the specification, quantity, and timeline requested — we do not bid on requirements we cannot fulfil to standard.

01

Quotation & specification review

We confirm requirements, quantities and delivery terms before submitting a bid or quote.

02

Sourcing & quality check

Goods are sourced from vetted suppliers and checked against the agreed specification.

03

Delivery & handover

We coordinate transport and delivery to site, with documentation for procurement records.

04

After‑delivery support

A direct contact remains available for any follow‑up, replacement or documentation needs.
